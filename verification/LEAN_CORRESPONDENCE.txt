Banded Totally Positive Matrices and Positive Bidiagonal Factorizations: Existence, Stability, and Toeplitz Orthogonality

Numbers and pages read from the current Normal and Memoirs AMS auxiliary files. Stable LaTeX labels and Lean declaration names identify the mathematics independently of numbering. No correspondence with a particular arXiv upload is asserted.

Fresh builds of all 74 mathematical source modules (29 + 42 + 3), followed by an axiom audit of every project theorem. Common modules are counted separately in the first two projects.

Index translation. In the semi-infinite modules, factors are numbered from zero on the right: a j n corresponds to the paper's hat a_(p-j)(n). The auxiliary index k for next minors has paper order k+1. inverseMinorRatio a p k n corresponds to kappa_(k+1,n+1). Consequently next_minor_at_zero is needed separately for the n=0 determinant identity. cumulative, ratioAt and normalizedTail represent pi, r and chi, respectively. Names containing old proposition numbers are stable code identifiers, not current manuscript numbers. In the stability modules, blockProduct w s N multiplies w(s+1),...,w(s+N); the initial row is zero. In the periodic adjacent-ratio module, w(t)=a(t+1)/b(t) represents manuscript gamma(t+1).
