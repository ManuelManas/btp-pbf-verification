All 188 listed modules were rebuilt from source in four fresh project directories. The transitive axiom audits passed for all 5346 theorem checks across the projects. All 52 declaration references in the 18-row manuscript correspondence were matched to the new audit logs.

Index translation. In the semi-infinite modules, factors are numbered from zero on the right: a j n corresponds to the paper's hat a_(p-j)(n). The auxiliary index k for next minors has paper order k+1. inverseMinorRatio a p k n corresponds to kappa_(k+1,n+1). Consequently next_minor_at_zero is needed separately for the n=0 determinant identity. cumulative, ratioAt and normalizedTail represent pi, r and chi, respectively. Names containing old proposition numbers are stable code identifiers, not current manuscript numbers. In the stability modules, blockProduct w s N multiplies w(s+1),...,w(s+N); the initial row is zero. In the periodic adjacent-ratio module, w(t)=a(t+1)/b(t) represents manuscript gamma(t+1). The general_stability product API numbers bidiagonal factors from the left. FactorOrderBridge and RelativeFactorOrder prove the exact reversal needed by the legacy inverse-entry API. Row zero is a dummy factor entry. Fixed variations begin at row p-i for zero-based factor i, preserving exactly p(p-1)/2 physical initial entries. Relative maps represent the exact entrywise error divided by its original positive entry.
