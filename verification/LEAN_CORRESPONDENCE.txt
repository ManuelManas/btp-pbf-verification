Index translation. In the semi-infinite modules, factors are numbered from zero on the right: a j n corresponds to the paper's hat a_(p-j)(n). The auxiliary index k for next minors has paper order k+1. inverseMinorRatio a p k n corresponds to kappa_(k+1,n+1). Consequently next_minor_at_zero is needed separately for the n=0 determinant identity. cumulative, ratioAt and normalizedTail represent pi, r and chi, respectively. Names containing old proposition numbers are stable code identifiers, not current manuscript numbers.
