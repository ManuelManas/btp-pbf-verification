Scope of this integration:
The finite identities specialize the AiM determinants to our Toeplitz
class and identify their first components with the two adjacent DK shifts.
The zero limits follow from DK. A direct proof establishes the alternating
real-semiaxis geometry for all Gamma_k from the negative symbol roots.
Fixed-component determinant ratios converge to the scalar Favard functions.
Two dominant Widom terms yield characteristic-polynomial oscillations and
the normalized determinantal sine limit. Repeated roots inside separated
groups are handled by grouped contour expressions. Only compact-interior
asymptotics are claimed. Neither uniqueness of a determinantal point
process nor a classification of every Gamma_k as a full ray is asserted.
This material has exact and numerical checks and is not Lean formalization.
