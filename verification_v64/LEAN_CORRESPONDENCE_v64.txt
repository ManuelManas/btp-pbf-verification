This release adds the exact constant mathfrak B of Lemma 2.16.
The previous release v48 proves a larger uniform constant sufficient
for the global theorem. Its proofs remain unchanged. The two new modules
SharpUniformMinors.lean and CompleteSharpResults.lean prove the bound
with the manuscript's stated maximum, following its nested-sum argument.

The files listed below belong to lean/semi_infinite/ unless stated otherwise.

INDEX CONVENTIONS

The Lean Metelmann factor index j starts at zero, from the right:
a j n is the manuscript's widehat a_(p-j)(n). cumulative a j n is pi_j(n).
ratioAt a k s is r_k(s), while adjacentTerm a k n is r_k(k+n).
adjacentSeries a k is the sum S_k when the positive series converges.
consecutiveMinor a p k n c is the minor with rows n,...,n+k-1 and
column indices c(0),...,c(k-1), in that order.
normalizedTail a p l n j represents chi_(l,j)(n) in the nested-sum proof.

LEMMA 2.16 — lem:global-inverse-minors

Part (1), leading minors, for all n >= 0 and k <= p:
  InverseMinors.lean: leading_minor_formula, leading_minor_pos.
  Delta_(k,n) = consecutiveMinor a p k n (fun j => j.val).

Part (1), the second minor, including n=0:
  AdjacentRatios.lean: normalized_next_minor.
  CompleteSharpResults.lean: next_minor_formula, next_minor_at_zero,
    next_minor_pos.
  Here Lean index k represents manuscript order k+1. The sum interval
  Ico (k+1) (n+k+1) corresponds to s=k+1,...,n+k. The raw determinant
  equals Delta times this sum; it is zero at n=0 and positive at n>=1.

Part (2), reciprocal partial sums, positive ratios, strict decrease and limits:
  AdjacentRatios.lean: inverseMinorRatio_eq_convergent,
    inverseMinorRatio_strictAnti, inverseMinorRatio_tendsto.
  SeriesLimits.lean: convergent_pos, endpoint_eq_inv_tsum,
    endpoint_eq_zero_iff_not_summable.
  inverseMinorRatio a p k n represents kappa_(k+1,n+1).
  seriesEndpoint a k is the actual limit: 1/S_k in the summable case
  and zero in the nonsummable case. The nonsummable case is handled
  separately because Lean's real-valued tsum is not an extended real sum.
  CompleteSharpResults.lean: inverse_minors_identities_and_limits
  assembles the determinant identities, boundary case, strict positivity,
  strict decrease and convergence in a single statement.

Part (3), the precise maximum in eq:global-minor-bound-constant:
  SharpUniformMinors.lean:
    consecutiveSeriesProduct a l j = product S_h for l+1 <= h <= j;
    manuscriptBoundValues a p = {1} union all these products with 0<=l<j<p;
    manuscriptBound a p is the maximum of that nonempty finite set.
    manuscriptBound_one proves the p=1 value is 1.

  The proof proceeds as in the manuscript:
    normalizedTail_le_consecutiveSeriesProduct bounds each nested sum;
    normalizedTail_le_manuscriptBound takes the finite maximum;
    uniform_inverse_minors_sharp applies the normalized determinant identity
      and the permutation expansion, giving |minor/Delta| <= k! mathfrak B^k.
  CompleteSharpResults.lean: inverse_minors_uniform_bound_complete.
  This holds for all k<=p, all n>=0, and every list of k column indices below p.
  Increasing lists representing k-element subsets are included.

The new estimate requires convergence of the adjacent series, exactly as in
part (3). It assumes neither the desired determinant bound nor a PBF.

VALIDATION

Run sh check.sh in lean/semi_infinite/. It creates an empty local output
directory and checks all 28 modules with warnings treated as errors.
The compilation record and validation.json document this release's run.
The new theorems and the global criterion use only propext,
Classical.choice and Quot.sound, the standard axioms reported by Lean.

All 68 Lean files from v48 are retained unchanged; two files are added.
The Toeplitz project is unchanged from that release. Python checks and
their v63 records are retained separately in computational/audit_v63/.
They check the finite cases, identities and numerical integrals specified
in their source; they are distinct from the Lean proofs listed above.
